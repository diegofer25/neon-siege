⚡ Neon Siege

Survive. Level up. Dominate.
A fast-paced wave survival shooter drenched in neon and synthwave style. Hold the line against relentless enemy hordes, build your character with deep RPG progression, and unleash devastating abilities to stay alive — one more wave.

🎮 Core Gameplay

  • Infinite Wave Survival — Face increasingly brutal waves of enemies that scale in health, speed, and damage. Every wave tests your build.
  • Smart Auto-Targeting — Your cannon tracks enemies intelligently. Focus on positioning, dodging, and build decisions — not pixel-perfect aim.
  • 5 Unique Boss Types — Chrono Boss (time manipulation), Shield Boss (barrier phases), Splitter Boss (divides on hit), Teleporter Boss (blink strikes), and Vortex Boss (gravity wells).
  • Special Enemies — Healers that restore their allies and Splitters that fragment into smaller threats keep you on your toes.

🧠 Deep Build System

5 RPG Attributes — Every level grants points to distribute across:

  • 💥 Strength — Bullet damage, explosion power, projectile speed
  • ⚡ Dexterity — Fire rate, turn speed, movement speed
  • ❤️ Vitality — Max HP, shields, regeneration
  • 🧠 Intelligence — Cooldown reduction, AoE radius
  • 🍀 Luck — Critical hits, loot quality

2 Playable Archetypes (more coming soon!):

  • 🎯 Gunner — Glass cannon DPS. Piercing shots, triple shot, critical mastery, overcharge bursts, and the devastating Aimbot Overdrive ultimate that locks onto every enemy at once.
  • ⚡ Technomancer — AoE destruction. Explosive rounds, chain hits, burn auras, volatile kills, and the Lightning Cascade ultimate that bounces between ALL enemies with escalating damage.

4-Tier Skill Trees — Each archetype has a full skill tree with passives, actives, and a powerful ultimate ability. Invest skill points to unlock deeper tiers and shape your playstyle.

Ascension System — Every 5 waves, choose 1 of 3 powerful ascension modifiers that stack throughout your run, creating unique synergies every game.

🎨 Neon Aesthetic

  • Synthwave-inspired visuals with glowing neon effects on every element
  • Dynamic particle systems — explosions, muzzle flashes, hit sparks
  • Screen shake and visual feedback that makes every hit feel impactful
  • Floating damage numbers and animated UI transitions
  • Original generated soundtrack and sound effects

🏆 Compete & Progress

  • Global Leaderboards — Climb the ranks and prove your survival skills
  • Achievements — Unlock milestones and track your mastery
  • Run Save/Load — Save mid-run and resume later; never lose progress

⚙️ Technical

  • Runs entirely in the browser — no downloads, no installs
  • Built with vanilla JavaScript & HTML5 Canvas
  • Optimized for 60fps with object pooling and adaptive performance
  • Responsive design — plays on desktop and scales to different screens
  • High DPI / Retina display support
